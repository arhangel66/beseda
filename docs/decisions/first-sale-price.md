**Why $39.99:** It asks less up front than MacWhisper's displayed €64 local-first Pro license, yet charges for Beseda's specific value: separate mic/system channels, on-device transcription and a local-summary default without mandatory recurring cloud spend ([product](../product/index.md)). Subscription annual equivalents are not comparable lifetime prices; free Meetily and free tiers make a $49+ untested entry harder to justify. This is a testable positioning judgment, not evidence of willingness to pay. No discount machinery is needed.

**First $500:** `ceil(500 / 39.99) = 13` fully paid US-list-price orders; 12 yield $479.88, 13 yield **$519.87 gross list-price charges**. This is not $500 of proceeds. [Apple's price setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-pricing/set-a-price/) uses a base country/region and automatically calculates other storefront prices; [pricing reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/pricing-and-availability/app-pricing-and-availability/) describes regional tiers, currency and tax adjustments. US $39.99 is a target tier to confirm in App Store Connect, not a promise that all regions pay $39.99 or that an exact tier is available. Sales taxes/VAT can be included or added depending on storefront; currency conversion, refunds and tax adjustments change reported gross and proceeds. [Apple's Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) offers **15% commission only if enrolled and eligible**; otherwise applicable standard fees may differ. Thirteen non-refunded US orders clear $500 in nominal list-price charges, not necessarily tax-exclusive gross or payout; count actual paid, non-refunded transactions against the chosen gross definition. Do not confuse Apple's proceeds (after commission and certain taxes) with customer gross.
