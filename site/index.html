A Mac app for work conversations

Remember what you agreed on in your last work call.

Beseda records calls on your Mac and transcribes them locally. After a call, search the transcript or create a summary with your own prompt. Before the next related call, revisit what was agreed last time.

Your call, your choices

Audio and transcription stay on your Mac. The built-in summary model can run locally. Text can leave your Mac if you use OpenRouter for summaries, call classification or live key points, connect to a remote LM Studio server, or configure a webhook. Models need an internet download; the direct build may also download its summary engine and app updates. Apple handles Store app updates. Read the privacy details.

Common questions

Does the recording go to the cloud?

Audio and local transcription stay on the Mac. Optional providers and webhooks can send text elsewhere; see Privacy.

Can I record any call?

You are responsible for recording lawfully, getting participants' consent and storing the data. Beseda does not notify other participants for you.

Can I use it for sensitive conversations?

Local-only mode and retention controls help, but they do not protect files from someone using your unlocked Mac, software running as you, or unencrypted backups. See Privacy.

Do I need internet?

Models need a download. The direct build may also download its summary engine and app updates; Apple handles Store app updates. Once a speech model is installed, transcription works locally; cloud features need a connection.
